About

Construction isn't just something I studied. It's something I grew up around. My grandfather built for a living, my father works in construction today, and I've carried that same work ethic into my own career. You could say it runs in the family.

I'm currently a student at Texas Tech University, and I bring more than two years of hands-on, on-site experience to every project. That time in the field taught me what can't be learned in a classroom: how a job site really runs, how to solve problems as they come up, and why doing the work right the first time matters.

Combining a strong family foundation in the trade with a college education, I'm focused on building a career that honors where I came from while bringing fresh ideas to the industry.
